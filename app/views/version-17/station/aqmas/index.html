Air Quality Management Areas (AQMAs)

Air Quality Management Areas (AQMAs) are areas where air quality objectives are not being met or are at risk of not being met.

Area name Pollutant Status
Birmingham AQMA Nitrogen dioxide (NO2) Active

Air Quality Action Plans (AQAP)

Local authorities must produce an air quality action plan (AQAP) for any places where there is an AQMA.

This is the plan that explains how they intend to bring air pollution down below the air quality objectives.
